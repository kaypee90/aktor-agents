# Regional rental market outlook 2026

*Prepared for property managers in the Lakeshore metro area. Figures are for professionally managed
mid-rise buildings (50–500 units).*

## Rents

- Asking rents for new leases rose **3.8%** over the last twelve months.
- The **average renewal increase** offered by large operators was **4.5%**. Operators offering
  more than **7%** lost noticeably more renewals than those offering less.
- Two new buildings opening in Q2 2026 add about **900 units** within two miles of the waterfront,
  including the Riverside district.

## Competition

- To fill the new buildings, competitors are offering a **$500 move-in credit** and one month free on
  13-month leases.
- Renters in the area report that the move-in credit is the most common reason they compare
  options before renewing.

## Renewals

- Across the metro area, the **typical renewal rate is 55–58%** for mid-rise buildings, lower than
  the 60–65% seen before 2024.
- Tenants who have lived in a unit for more than five years renew far more often than first-year
  tenants, regardless of the increase.
- Unresolved maintenance requests are the second most common reason tenants give for leaving,
  after price.

## Outlook

Occupancy across the metro area is expected to stay between **91% and 94%** through 2026, with the
usual summer peak, but new supply may push it toward the bottom of that range in the second half
of the year.
