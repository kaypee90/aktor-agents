# Building and running it: cost notes (sample data)

*Illustrative figures for the Aktor Studies walkthrough.*

## Building the first version

- Two engineers for **five to seven months** (an SMS/WhatsApp intake, triage with an LLM, a
  contractor directory, a landlord dashboard). Loaded cost **$13,000 per engineer per month**.
- Design and legal review (terms, data processing): about **$15,000** once.

## Running it

- LLM and messaging costs: **$0.60–$1.40 per landlord per month** at 10–15 requests.
- Payment fees: **3%** of revenue.
- Support: one person part time from launch (**$3,500 a month**), full time past ~800 customers.
- Hosting and tools: **$1,200 a month**.
- After launch the team shrinks to one engineer (**$13,000 a month**).

## Constraints and risks

- Contractor booking needs a vetted contractor network per city; partners take 4–6 weeks to
  onboard in each metro area.
- Some states require a licence to *arrange* repairs for a fee; booking must stay a referral.
- Messaging providers need carrier registration (about four weeks) before sending texts at volume.
