# Maintenance triage

1. **Category.** Water, drains, toilets, taps → plumbing. Sockets, lights, breakers → electrical.
   Radiators, boilers, thermostats → heating. Fridges, ovens, washers → appliance. Anything else → other.
2. **Priority.**
   - **urgent**: active water leak, no heating when it's below 10°C outside, gas smell, no power to the unit,
     a door or window that won't lock.
   - **normal**: something broken that affects daily life but can wait a day or two.
   - **low**: cosmetic or minor (a squeaky hinge, a scuffed wall).
3. **Notes.** Say what you decided and why in two or three sentences a technician can act on: which unit,
   what's wrong, and anything the resident said about access.
