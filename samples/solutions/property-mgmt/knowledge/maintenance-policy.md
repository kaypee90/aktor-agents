# Maintenance policy

- Urgent requests get a technician within 4 hours, day or night. The on-call manager is told at once.
- Normal requests are scheduled within 2 working days.
- Low-priority requests are batched into the monthly maintenance round.
- Residents are never charged for repairs caused by wear and tear. Damage caused by a resident is
  charged at cost, and only after the manager confirms it.
- Pest control is always normal priority, unless it affects more than one unit (then urgent).
