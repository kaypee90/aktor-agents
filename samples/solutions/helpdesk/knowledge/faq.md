# Product FAQ

**How do I reset my password?** Settings → Security → Reset password. A link arrives by email within a minute.

**How do I export my data?** Reports → Export. CSV exports are limited to 50,000 rows; use the API for more.

**How do I add a teammate?** Settings → Members → Invite. Team and Enterprise plans only.

**Which plan am I on?** Settings → Billing shows the plan and the next invoice.
