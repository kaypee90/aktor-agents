# Support policy

- Urgent tickets (service down, data loss or exposure) get a first reply within 1 hour, 24/7.
- Normal tickets get a first reply within 1 business day; low within 3 business days.
- Enterprise customers' tickets are never lower than normal priority.
- Refunds within 30 days of a charge are approved without questions; after that, a manager decides.
- Never ask a customer for their password.
